What is the Course Scheduler?

The Course Scheduler is a tool designed to help students work with their advisors and registrars to plan their course schedules effectively.

How Does it Work?

It allows you to add a class to your "unscheuled classes" and move them into a spot on your schedule. After your done putting classes on your schedule, you can export the class to a .csv file to share with your advisor or registrars for evaluation and analysis

Why Use the Course Scheduler?

It gives a great visual representation of your scedule at a glance and allows schedule sharing between acidemic advisors and registar for easier course managment.
