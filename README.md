# simple_site

This is a simple, one page site for a class assignment. This project is based off of a project I am working on allowing students to schedule their classes and work together with advisors and registrar office at their school.

# Requirements for the project:

### HTML:

1. At least 5 different semetic HTML elements
1. At least one image with proper alt text
1. At least one form element
1. At least 3 distinct sections of content

### CSS:

1. At least 10 CSS rulesets
1. Use of classs and IDs to target specific elements
1. At least one media query to make your site responsive
1. Thoughtful color scheme, spacing and typography

### JavaScript:

1. At least 2 event listeners
1. DOM manipulation that changes the page content or styling based on user interaction
1. At least one function that encapsulates your logic
